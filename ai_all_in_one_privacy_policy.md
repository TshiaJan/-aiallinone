Thank you for using **AI All-In-One Platform** ("the App", "we", "our", or "us"), developed by Tshiamo Jantjie. This Privacy Policy details how we treat data and user privacy when you interact with our application.

---

## 1. Overview

**AI All-In-One Platform** is an idle simulation and empire tycoon management game designed for offline and casual entertainment.
- We do **not** collect, store, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII).
- No user accounts, passwords, email addresses, or social logins are required.
- Game progress, unlocked platforms, power upgrades, and virtual revenue stats are processed and maintained solely on your local device.

---

## 2. Information We Process

### A. Local Storage & Device Data
The App uses local browser/webview storage () on your device solely to:
- Persist unlocked platform levels (across all 26 AI business divisions)
- Save current reality tier, power upgrades, and prestige achievements
- Preserve user preferences (sound, animations, and visual toggles)

All of this data is strictly client-side and never transmitted to our servers. Clearing your application storage or uninstalling the app permanently erases this local data.

### B. Network & Telemetry
The core application runs completely offline without requiring continuous network connectivity. We do not track user keystrokes, GPS location, contacts, or personal files.

### C. Optional Advertising & Monetization
If the App integrates standard advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob) for optional rewarded boosts:
- These third-party networks may collect anonymous, non-personal device identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID) solely to deliver non-intrusive ads, prevent ad fraud, and provide frequency capping.
- Please refer to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for details on their data processing practices.

## 3. Children's Privacy

The App is designed for a general audience and is family-friendly. We do not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under 13 (or 16 in applicable European jurisdictions), in strict adherence to COPPA and GDPR regulations.

## 4. Third-Party Integrations

The App operates independently and does not link to third-party social networks, tracking pixels, or data brokers.

## 5. Security

Because all simulation state and gameplay metrics remain strictly on your physical device, your data is as secure as your personal hardware.
